Norway's Next Match

Norway beat Brazil 2-1 (Erling Haaland scored both goals, 79' and 90') in the Round of 16 on July 5, 2026 — their first-ever World Cup quarterfinal appearance.

Quarterfinal — Saturday, July 11, 2026

Kickoff: 5:00 PM ET / 11:00 PM Swedish time (CEST)

Venue: Hard Rock Stadium, Miami Gardens, FL

Opponent: England (confirmed) — England beat co-host Mexico 3-2 on July 5 (Jude Bellingham scored twice 98 seconds apart, Harry Kane converted a penalty while England were down to 10 men).
